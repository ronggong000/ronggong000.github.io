Research

Research interests

My work is organized around three connected questions.

01

Motion representation

How can models learn transferable temporal structure from unlabeled human video and synthetic 3D data?

02

Motion generation

How can we generate controllable body, hand, and facial motion while preserving fine-grained behavior?

03

Embodied learning

How can human motion and video provide scalable training signals for agents that perceive and act?
