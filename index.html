WACV 2027 · Accepted 3D motion · Sign language

MV-Sign: Multi-View 3D Motion Integration and Enhanced Text-to-Motion Conditioning for Sign Language Production

Singyu Muk, Kaustubh Kundu, Kunyuan Xie, Weijie Wang, Kalin Stefanov

A multi-view curation pipeline for high-fidelity 3D signing data, paired with sign-aware motion tokenization, pseudo-gloss conditioning, and keyframe-aware retrieval. The system reduces body and hand DTW-PA-JPE by 32.8% and 20.9% over reported prior work.

S2HAND: Accuracy-Preservation Trade-Offs for Hand Specialization in Whole-Body Pose Models

Singyu Muk, Xuanren Song, Kalin Stefanov

Hand specialization for Sapiens2-5B through protected-output adaptation and Fisher-selected Transformer tuning, improving difficult hand pose while retaining the unified 308-keypoint interface and non-hand behavior.
