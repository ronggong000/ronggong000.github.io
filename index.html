Personal profile

Biography

I am an AI researcher interested in how machines reconstruct, represent, and generate motion from human video. My work connects 3D human motion, sign language, whole-body pose estimation, video generative models, and scalable learning for embodied agents.

At Monash Assistive Tech, I develop data pipelines and learning methods for fine-grained body and hand motion. My current research studies transferable facial and embodied motion representations inside video foundation models.
